## Works I could not fully resolve, and what would settle them

1. **beach.html — who is she?** Long blonde hair, tiara-style headband, violet eyes, heart sunglasses, black bikini. Reads as Mythra (Xenoblade Chronicles 2) but nothing on the page names her and the panels carry no text. *Needs:* the artist's own title for the Drive folder, or the original post caption.
2. **dc1.html — title says "Harley & Poison" but Poison Ivy is not in it.** All 15 gallery panels plus the splice contact sheet are Harley Quinn bust variants. *Needs:* a decision — either retitle dc1 to "Harley" and let dc2 carry the pairing, or confirm an Ivy set exists in the Drive folder that was never uploaded.
3. **blackgold.html — is "Gwen" a character?** The three chapters are the same 10 renders with three head coverings: "Panel Gag", "Harness Gag", "Gwen Hood". "Gwen Hood" may name a hood style rather than the woman wearing it. *Needs:* the artist's naming intent. The woman herself is never named in-image.
4. **dovah.html — Skyrim link unproven.** *Dovah* is the Dragon-language word for dragon, and she has ram horns, but the outfit is a teal harem-style set with no Elder Scrolls iconography. *Needs:* confirmation. Suggest tagging it `fantasy` and leaving franchise blank until then.
5. **firelord.html — Avatar link unproven.** The gold flame finial on the hood matches a Fire Nation royal headpiece and the title is "Fire Lord", but the character (bespectacled, red latex catsuit) resembles no Avatar character. Most likely an original character in a Fire Nation-styled costume. *Needs:* confirmation.
6. **bulletgirl.html — original character or the Fawcett/DC Bulletgirl?** The red top, yellow shorts and chrome bullet helmet track the comics character closely, but nothing on the page says so. *Needs:* confirmation before adding a DC franchise tag alongside dc1/dc2/harleyraven.
7. **corridor.html and disgust.html — Raven is inferred, never named.** Purple bob, violet eyes, red diamond on the forehead. Strong read, but no on-panel name in any of the 16 panels sampled. *Needs:* confirmation, or tag as "unnamed purple-haired woman (Raven-styled)".
8. **kyra1.html / kyra2.html — which figure is Kyra?** Four captives in Kyra, two in Kyra II, none identified in-image. *Needs:* the artist to point at one.
9. **pack1.html vs pantied.html — two different Jinxes.** Mummy Pack 01's Jinx has lavender hair and a collar; PanTied's Jinx has blue braids (an Arcane/LoL read). Either they are unrelated characters sharing a name, or one is a redesign. *Needs:* clarification before the character index links them.
10. **whale.html / renders.html — the existing `characters` meta is wrong.** Both pages carry `characters="Donna, Jane"`. The on-panel cast is Jane, Judi, Peter, Carol, Abigail and Soldier #04; **Donna appears only in animation.html** (four "Donna" clips). The meta looks copied from the animation page. *Needs:* a correction pass on those two meta tags.
11. **Duplicate content that inflates counts.**
    - `boundborne.html` chapter 4 ("J&J Preview") is the same 88 panels as `jjp.html` — 213 vs 125 genuinely new.
    - `perilvore.html` chapters "By stage" and "By camera" are the same ~99 renders sorted two ways — 199 vs ~99 unique.
    - `boundborne-full.html` is an offline single-file build of BoundBorne with 125 base64-embedded JPEGs; it has no `window.CONFIG` and is not counted as a separate work.
    Decide whether the gallery's "N Images" figures should count unique renders or listed entries.
12. **Pages in the gallery that are not readers** (no `scrubZone`, so out of scope for this table but worth a tagging decision):
    - `aislinne.html` — an 84 MB fantasy-RPG script/dialogue archive (trap scenes, worm den, pods, doppel-suits). Text, not images. Themes visible from the section names: fantasy RPG, traps, rubber pods, worms, doppelgänger suits.
    - `animation.html` — 16 Sperm Whale clips (4 Donna, 12 Jane). Format would be **animation**; it is the only animation work in the archive.
    - `spermwhale.html` — a hub page linking whale / renders / animation.
13. **afflatus.html has no `driveUrl` and no meta tags**, unlike almost every other work. Also its eight chapters mix three different things (two story chapters, a superseded 94-panel "Old Version", teaser/exclusive stills, and two 16-panel character turnarounds). *Needs:* a decision on whether Format should be a single value per work or per chapter.
